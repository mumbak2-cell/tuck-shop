Making a Sale

1
Find the product — Browse category tabs, use search, or scroll. Toggle between grid and list view using the button next to search.
2
Add to cart — Tap a product to add it. Use the +/− buttons to adjust quantity, or tap the number and type a quantity directly.
3
Tap Pay — Opens the payment screen.
4
Choose payment method — Cash (with change calculation), Card, Mobile Money, EFT, or Credit Account.
5
Receipt — Print, or share via WhatsApp. A 6-character receipt code is shown for lookup later.

Credit Sales

When a customer wants to buy on credit, select Credit Account as the payment method. Pick the customer from your list. Tilify checks their credit limit before allowing the sale.

Low Stock Warnings

Products with 5 or fewer units show an amber "N left" badge on their tile. At zero stock, the product disappears from the grid.

Promotions

Active promotions apply automatically. You'll see the discount amount and promotional price on affected items in the cart.

ⓘ
The POS works fully offline. Sales queue up and sync automatically when your connection returns.

Opening a Shift

1
Navigate to Shift in the sidebar.
2
Enter your opening float (the cash you start with in the drawer).
3
Tap Open Shift. You can now make sales on the POS.

Closing a Shift

1
Go to Shift and tap Close Shift.
2
Count your cash using the denomination counter (notes and coins). The total is calculated automatically.
3
Review the difference between expected cash and counted cash.
4
Tap Close Shift to complete.
ⓘ

⚠
Stock count required: If enabled in Settings, cashiers must complete a stock count before closing their shift.

Voiding a Sale

Expand a receipt, select the item(s) to void, and confirm. The stock is automatically returned. Voiding is blocked on period-locked dates.

Processing a Return

From a receipt, tap Return on individual items. A credit note is generated with a CN-YYMMDD-HHMM-XXXX reference. For credit sales, the customer's balance is reduced.

09

Receive Stock

Record deliveries from suppliers. Stock levels are updated immediately. Attach a photo of the supplier invoice for your records.

1
Go to Receive Stock and select the Supplier. You can add new suppliers inline.
2
Add lines: choose the product, enter quantity, unit cost, and pack size.
3
Optionally attach a receipt photo (PDF, JPEG, PNG, or WebP, max 5 MB).
4
Tap Save. Stock is added to your current branch. An expense entry is optionally created.
ⓘ
Prepared food toggle: When receiving ingredients for prepared items, switch on "Prepared food?" to skip creating an expense entry (ingredient costs flow through recipes instead).
10

Stock Count

Count physical stock on your shelves. A cashier submits a count; the owner reviews and applies it to update stock levels.

1
Go to Stock Count. All products appear with their expected quantities.
2
Enter the counted quantity for each product. Use search to find specific items.
3
Tap Save Count. This saves a count session without changing actual stock.
4
Owner review: The owner sees pending count sessions and taps "Confirm and apply to stock" to update stock levels.
⚠
Only the shop owner (logged in with both the owner account and Admin PIN) can confirm and apply counts. Saving a count never changes stock levels directly — even when the owner does the counting.
11

Stock Adjustments

Record non-sale stock changes: breakage, expired items, theft, damage, samples, or corrections.

1
Go to Stock Adjustments and tap New Adjustment.
2
Select the product, choose a reason (Breakage, Expired, Theft, Damaged, Samples, Correction, Other).
3
Choose increase or decrease and enter the quantity. Add notes if needed.
4
Save. Stock is updated immediately. Loss-category adjustments appear on your Profit & Loss report as shrinkage.

Stock Transfers

Move stock between branches. Requires two or more locations.

1
Go to Stock Transfers and tap New Transfer.

Select the source and destination branches.
3
Add items and quantities. Tilify checks that sufficient stock exists at the source.
4
Review and confirm. A transfer receipt is generated that you can print, download as PDF, email, or share via WhatsApp.

Revenue Assurance

Reconcile stock movement against recorded sales. Identify unrecorded shrinkage (items left the shelf without a sale) and oversells (more rung up than stock explains).

1
Do a stock count first, so Tilify has a closing figure to compare against.
2
Go to Revenue Assurance. Select the stock count session to compare.
3
Review each product: opening stock + replenished − closing stock = expected sales. Compare to recorded sales.
4
Products flagged with discrepancies need investigation: either stock walked out, or the count was wrong.
⚠
Revenue Assurance is only as accurate as your stock counts. Count regularly for best results.

Key Settings

  • Shift enforcement — Require an open shift before the POS allows sales.
  • Blind cash-up — Hides expected cash from cashiers during shift close.
  • Require stock count — Cashiers must complete a stock count before closing their shift.
  • Cashier credit sales — Toggle whether cashiers can make credit sales or if it's admin-only.
  • Wholesale pricing — Enable per-branch wholesale mode with minimum quantities.
  • Cash back — Allow cash back against card payments.
  • Card reward — Offer a free item when electronic payment exceeds a threshold.
  • Daily digest — Receive automated daily sales reports by email.
  • Period lock — Lock historical dates to prevent any modifications (voids, edits, deletions).
  • Prepared food — Enable ingredient tracking and recipe costing.
  • Warehouse (WMS) — Enable the warehouse management module.

Team & Permissions

Add staff, assign them to branches, and control what each person can access.

Adding Staff

1
Go to Settings → Team and tap Add Staff.
2
Enter their name, email, and set a temporary password.
3
Choose their role: Cashier or Manager.
4
Assign a branch (required for cashiers). They will be forced to change their password on first login.

Manager Permissions

Owners can grant or revoke individual permissions for each manager:

  • Manage suppliers
  • Manage locations
  • Manage stock transfers
  • Manage expenses
  • Manage payment methods
  • View reports
  • Void sales
  • Manage blind cash-up
  • Manage warehouse (WMS)
  • Manage shift admin (reopen/delete shifts)
ⓘ
You can switch a staff member between Cashier and Manager without removing and re-adding them. Use the role switcher on their profile.
21

Locations

Manage multiple branches. Each location has its own stock levels, PINs, and shift history.

  • Starter plan — 1 location
  • Growth plan — Up to 3 locations
  • Pro plan — Unlimited locations

Switch between locations using the location dropdown in the sidebar. All data (sales, stock, shifts) is scoped to the currently selected location.

Per-Branch Pricing

Charge different prices at different branches. In the product form, expand "Prices per branch" (visible only when you have 2+ locations) and set override prices for specific branches. Any branch without an override uses the base price.

Warehouse Management (WMS)

For shops with a separate warehouse or storage facility. Enable in Settings → Warehouse. Available on the Pro plan.

The Warehouse module adds a separate set of screens under a Warehouse section in the sidebar:

  • WMS Dashboard — Warehouse overview: stock levels, recent activity, alerts.
  • Warehouse Stock — Catalog of warehouse items with SKU, category, pack size, and ABC classification. Link items to retail products.
  • Receive — Receive goods into the warehouse.
  • Dispatch — Pick, pack, and ship stock from warehouse to retail locations.
  • Transfers — Move stock between warehouses.
  • Adjustments — Warehouse-level stock adjustments.
  • Stock Count — Warehouse-level physical counts.
  • Purchase Orders — Create and track orders to suppliers.
  • Bin Locations — Manage storage locations within the warehouse.
ⓘ
WMS items can be linked to retail products. When you dispatch from warehouse to a branch, the retail product stock is updated automatically.
24

Offline Mode

Tilify is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Core features work without an internet connection.

What works offline

  • Point of Sale — Browse products, make sales, process payments. Sales queue and sync when you reconnect.
  • Shift management — Open and close shifts.
  • Expenses — Record expenses. They sync later.
  • Customer adds — Add new customers. They sync later.

What requires connection

  • Reports and analytics
  • Settings changes
  • Team management
  • Receipt attachments
  • Warehouse (WMS) operations
ⓘ
An offline indicator appears in the header when you lose connection. Queued actions sync automatically when your connection returns — no action needed from you.
