Getting Started

Tilify is a web-based point-of-sale (POS) and inventory management app built for retail shops across Southern Africa. It runs in your browser on any device — phone, tablet, or computer — with no software to install.

Here's what happens when you use Tilify:

  • Sell — Ring up sales on a visual product grid. Accept cash, card, mobile money, or credit account payments. Receipts are generated with a unique code for lookup.
  • Track stock — Every sale automatically deducts stock at your branch. Receive deliveries, transfer between branches, count physical stock, and track adjustments (breakage, theft, expired).
  • Manage shifts — Cashiers open and close shifts with denomination-level cash counts. Blind cash-up ensures honest counting.
  • See your profit — Real-time dashboard, daily sales reports, full Profit & Loss statements, and Revenue Assurance to catch discrepancies between stock and sales.
  • Work offline — Tilify is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Sales, expenses, and shift operations continue without internet and sync automatically when the connection returns.

Your data is stored securely in the cloud and accessible from any device where you sign in.

Tilify is designed for retail shop owners in Southern Africa (SADC) — tuck shops, grocery stores, convenience stores, hardware shops, takeaways, and any business that sells physical products over a counter.

It works especially well for:

  • Single shops wanting professional POS without expensive hardware
  • Multi-branch operators who need consolidated reporting
  • Shops in areas with unreliable internet (offline-first design)
  • Businesses offering customer credit accounts
  • Food businesses that prepare items from ingredients

Getting started takes about 10 minutes:

  • Sign up at tilify.mkglobal.co.za with your email. No credit card needed.
  • Set up your shop — Enter your business name, currency, and basic settings.
  • Add your products — Name, selling price, cost price, and category. You can add stock quantities per branch.
  • Start selling — Your 14-day free trial begins immediately with full access to all features.

If you're importing from another system, contact us and we can help migrate your product catalogue.

Features

Yes. Tilify is a Progressive Web App (PWA) with full offline support. When the internet drops:

  • Sales continue — All transactions queue locally and sync when the connection returns.
  • Shift operations work normally — open and close shifts, count cash.
  • Expenses can be recorded offline.
  • New customers can be added.

An offline indicator appears in the header. Everything syncs automatically — don't sign out while offline.

Yes. On the Growth plan (up to 3 branches) or Pro plan (unlimited):

  • Each branch has its own stock levels, shifts, and sales records.
  • Products are shared across branches, with optional per-branch pricing for items that cost differently at different locations.
  • Cashiers are assigned to a specific branch and can only sell there.
  • Managers and owners see consolidated reports or filter by branch.
  • Stock can be transferred between branches with full tracking.

Tilify has a built-in customer credit system:

  • Add customers with a name, phone number, and optional credit limit.
  • At checkout, choose Credit Account and select the customer. The sale is recorded against their balance.
  • Track outstanding balances on the Customers page. Record payments as customers settle.
  • The Credit Ledger shows full transaction history per customer.

Credit sales access for cashiers is optional — the owner can enable or disable it in Settings.

Tilify provides comprehensive business intelligence:

  • Dashboard — Today's sales, top products, revenue trends at a glance.
  • Today's Sales — Receipt-level detail of every sale, expandable to line items. Void and return from here.
  • Reports — Sales by category, payment method breakdown, daily/weekly/monthly trends.
  • Profit & Loss — Full P&L statement with revenue, cost of goods sold, shrinkage, operating expenses, and net profit. VAT-registered businesses see ex-VAT figures with a VAT summary.
  • Revenue Assurance — Reconciles sales vs. stock movement to detect unrecorded sales, overselling, and shrinkage.
  • Daily email reports — Automated summary sent to the owner every morning.

Revenue Assurance is Tilify's stock-vs-sales reconciliation tool. It answers the question: "Did every product that left my shelf get rung up?"

  • Compares opening stock + deliveries against closing stock + sales.
  • Flags unrecorded units (stock left the shelf but no sale was recorded — possible theft or wastage).
  • Flags oversold units (more rung up than stock movement explains — indicates a counting error).
  • Calculates estimated missing revenue in Rands.

This is the feature owners use to hold their shop accountable. Available on all plans.

The Warehouse Management System is for businesses that have a central warehouse or distribution centre separate from their retail locations. It includes:

  • Warehouse-specific stock tracking with bin locations
  • Receive and dispatch workflows
  • Purchase orders for suppliers
  • Warehouse-to-shop transfers
  • Warehouse stock counts and adjustments

WMS is available on all plans. Enable it in Settings → Warehouse.

Operations

Go to Products → Add Product. For each product you enter:

  • Name and category (e.g. Drinks, Snacks, Bread)
  • Selling price — what the customer pays
  • Cost price — what you pay (package price ÷ quantity in pack)
  • Reorder level — get alerts when stock drops below this, per branch
  • Optional: per-branch price overrides, barcode/SKU

Products appear in the POS grid immediately. Stock is managed per branch — add quantities via Receive Stock.

A product's Inventory ID doubles as its barcode: edit it on an existing product to match what's printed on the packaging, and a USB or Bluetooth scanner will find and add it directly from the POS search bar — no separate scan mode needed.

Go to Settings → Team:

  • Tap Add Team Member. Enter their name and email. They'll receive a login with a temporary password.
  • Choose their role: Cashier (POS, shift, stock count, expenses) or Manager (everything except billing).
  • Assign cashiers to a specific branch — they can only sell at that location.
  • For managers, you can fine-tune permissions (e.g. allow reports but not voiding sales).

Each branch has a shared 4-digit PIN for the till (separate admin and cashier PINs). Team members sign in with email/password first, then enter the branch PIN. Optionally, an owner can set a personal PIN for each team member in Settings → Team, so till actions are attributed to that person by name instead of just "admin" or "cashier".

Yes. After every sale, Tilify generates a receipt with a 6-character code (e.g. 3F9A2C). You can:

  • Print to any connected printer (thermal receipt printers work best)
  • Share via WhatsApp — send the receipt as an image to the customer
  • Look up any receipt later using its code on the Sales page

Receipts show your shop name, items, prices, payment method, and date/time.

Stock counts in Tilify follow a two-step approval process:

  • Count — Any team member (cashier or manager) goes to Stock Count, enters actual quantities for each product, and taps Save. This creates a pending count session.
  • Approve — Only the shop owner can review and confirm a pending count. Confirming updates the stock levels in the system.

This prevents a cashier from silently adjusting stock to hide losses. The count is recorded but doesn't change anything until the owner approves it.

Optionally, you can require a stock count before a shift can be closed (enable in Settings).

Go to Reorder List — it shows every product at or below its reorder level at your current branch. From there you can:

  • Filter by supplier or category, tick the items to order, and adjust quantities (entered in packs)
  • Send the order via WhatsApp, PDF, or Copy
  • Tap Create PO to save it as a Purchase Order with its own number

When the delivery arrives, go to Receive Stock and tap Load from PO to pull in the supplier, items, and quantities automatically — no retyping. Saving the receipt marks the PO as received and generates a Goods Received Note you can also share via WhatsApp, PDF, or Copy from the receipt's History.

Both are managed on the Promotions page:

  • Promotions — a percentage discount on one product that applies automatically whenever it's in the cart, for a chosen date range
  • Combos — a fixed price for a specific pair of products, added to the cart as one deliberate tap at the till (e.g. "Bread & Butter" for a set price)

A combo tile only appears at the POS when both products have stock at the current branch.

Yes. If your business is VAT-registered, enter your VAT percentage in Settings. Tilify then:

  • Calculates all revenue and COGS figures ex-VAT on the Profit & Loss page
  • Shows a VAT Summary card with output VAT collected, input VAT on stock purchases, and net VAT payable
  • Assumes all selling prices are VAT-inclusive (standard in SADC markets)

Non-VAT-registered businesses see raw figures with no VAT breakdown.

Security & Support

Yes. Tilify takes data security seriously:

  • Encryption in transit — All data is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Row-level security — Your data is isolated at the database level. No other business can see or access your records.
  • PIN-based till access — Separate admin and cashier PINs protect till operations even on shared devices.
  • Permission controls — Owners can restrict what each manager can see and do.
  • Immutable sales records — Sales cannot be edited or deleted. Voids and returns create new audit-trailed records.
  • Period locking — Lock past periods to prevent changes to historical data.

Data is hosted on Supabase infrastructure with automatic backups. Tilify is built by MK Global SA (Pty) Ltd, registered in South Africa (2025/522597/07).
