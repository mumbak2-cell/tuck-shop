Cashier Quick-Start

Tilify Cashier Guide

Everything you need to sell, count stock, and close your shift. Takes about 2 minutes to read.

01

Logging In

Your shop owner will give you an email address and a temporary password. Use these to sign in at tilify.mkglobal.co.za.

1
Open Tilify in your browser and enter your email and temporary password.
2
Change your password — You will be asked to set a new password on your first login. Choose something you'll remember.
3
After changing your password, you'll see the PIN pad.
⚠
You must change your password before you can access anything. This is for security.
02

PIN Entry

After logging in, enter the Cashier PIN for your branch. Your manager will tell you this PIN.

03

Your Screens

As a cashier, you have access to these screens in the sidebar:

  • Shift — Open and close your shift
  • Point of Sale — Ring up sales
  • Stock Count — Count physical stock
  • Expenses — Record business expenses
  • Customers — View customers and record payments (if enabled by your manager)
  • Credit Ledger — See credit history (if enabled by your manager)

Other screens like Dashboard, Products, Reports, and Settings are for your manager/owner only.

How to Ring Up a Sale

1
Find the product — Tap a category tab or type in the search box.
2
Tap the product to add it to the cart. Tap again to add more, or use the +/− buttons.
3
Change quantity — Tap the number in the cart and type the exact amount you need.
4
Remove an item — Tap the trash icon next to it.
5
When ready, tap Pay.
ⓘ
Items with an amber "3 left" badge are running low. At zero stock, they disappear from the grid.
ⓘ
Use the grid/list toggle (next to search) to switch to a list view if product names are long.
05

Taking Payment

After tapping Pay, a payment screen appears. Choose how the customer is paying.

Cash

1
Tap Cash.
2
Enter the amount the customer gives you. Change is calculated automatically.
3
Confirm the sale. A receipt is generated.

Card / Mobile Money / EFT

Tap the matching button. Process the payment on your card machine or phone, then confirm in Tilify.

After Payment

A receipt appears with a 6-character code (like 3F9A2C). You can:

  • Print the receipt (if a printer is connected)
  • Share via WhatsApp — send to the customer
06

Credit Sales

If your manager has enabled credit sales for cashiers, you can sell on credit.

1
On the payment screen, tap Credit Account.
2
Select the customer from the list.
3
Tilify checks their credit limit. If they're within limits, the sale goes through.
⚠
If you don't see the Credit Account option, your manager hasn't enabled credit sales for cashiers. Ask them to enable it in Settings.
07

Opening a Shift

You may need to open a shift before the POS lets you sell (depends on your shop's settings).

1
Go to Shift in the sidebar.
2
Enter your opening float — the cash already in the drawer.
3
Tap Open Shift. You're ready to sell.
08

09

Stock Count

Count the physical stock on the shelves. Your count is saved for the owner to review — it does not change stock levels directly.

1
Go to Stock Count in the sidebar.
2
All products appear with their expected quantities. Use search to find specific items.
3
Enter the actual quantity you counted for each product.
4
Tap Save Count. Your count is submitted for review.
⚠
Be accurate. Stock counts feed into the shop's revenue assurance and profit calculations. Count carefully.
10

Expenses

Record money spent during your shift (transport, fuel, consumables, etc.).

1
Go to Expenses and tap Record Expense.
2
Pick a category (Transport, Fuel, Consumables, etc.).
3
Enter the amount and a short description.
4
Optionally snap a photo of the receipt.
ⓘ
Expenses work offline. They sync when the internet comes back.
11

Customers

If your manager has enabled customer access for cashiers, you can view customer profiles and record payments.

  • View balance — See how much a customer owes.
  • Record payment — When a customer pays off their balance, tap Payment and enter the amount.
  • Add customer — Add new customers with their name and phone number.
12

Offline Mode

If the internet goes down, keep working. These features work without a connection:

  • Point of Sale — All sales are saved and will sync later.
  • Shift — Open and close shifts.
  • Expenses — Record expenses.
  • Add customers — New customers sync when you reconnect.

An offline indicator appears in the header when you're disconnected. Everything syncs automatically when the connection returns — you don't need to do anything.

ⓘ
Don't sign out when offline. Your session stays active and sales keep queuing.
